Judgment:
- The kernel's copies are not the cost: AMD's are faster than the T14's.
- The userland copy is. AMD's is 2.5 us slower per 8 KiB frame and 1.3 us per
  4 KiB frame, which is the regression: about 16 frames x 2.5 us at 65536, and
  two at 4096 against +1.9 us.
- So by the rule the fix is a receive into the caller's own buffer, with no
  copy out of a FrameRx: c715ebfb5.
- Separately, userland copy_from_slice on the AMD bench runs at about 3 GB/s
  against the T14's 50, while the kernel's copies there are fast.
