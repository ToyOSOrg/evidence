## The T14's single-thread gap (corrects r5)

- **Measured:** ToyOS's T14 tops out at 3,788-3,789 MHz in every `mhz` and `cpu.clock` line of its boots.
  Linux reaches 4,189-4,190 MHz, near Intel's 4.20 GHz Max Turbo for the i5-1135G7. That ratio, 1.106,
  accounts for most of ToyOS's 1.10-1.17x on single-thread deflate, sha256 and matmul.
- **Not measured: why ToyOS stops 0.4 GHz short.** r5 called this "a clock difference, not a code
  difference". That was wrong: the ceiling is ToyOS's own open weakness. It belongs to "The T14's clock under
  load" in `issues/toyos-uses-what-modern-hardware-offers-for-speed.md`, which records that what holds the
  T14's clock is unread.
- **Inferred, not measured:**
  - That issue records ToyOS's idle cores never going below C1/C1E
    (`issues/idle-cpus-enter-the-c-states-cst-names.md`).
  - The one limit bit it logged is multi-core turbo, whose ceiling depends on how many cores are active.
  - So ToyOS's idle policy may itself be what holds one busy core below the single-core turbo bin.
