wifi_s1_compat_format - GREEN expected. The source is the Type-C xHCI 00:0d.0 [8086:9a13], left to a claim and driven
by usbd, whose bring-up No-Op raises the claim's message.
  A: "iommu: claim slot N's message for 00:0d.0 left in compatibility format (claim-compat-format): address=0xfee00000 data=0x000000VV";
     "PCI 00:0d.0: msi address=0xfee00000 ..." read back off the device, bit 4 clear (compatibility format);
     every unit "ires=y cfis=n eime=y";
     "iommu: DMA FAULT owner=slotN unit3 stream=00:0d.0 ... reason=0x25 ... compatibility-format-interrupt-blocked";
     NO "pcidev: slot N took its first message"; usbd ends on the claim's refusal ("the claim refused its interrupt record (Io)"),
     the job says "usbd ended before it settled". The boot hands the machine back: a claim's fault is its holder's, not the machine's.
  B: "PCI 00:0d.0: msi address=0xfee0XXXX ..." with bit 4 set (remappable); "pcidev: slot N took its first message on vector ...";
     no DMA FAULT naming 00:0d.0; usbd "a No-Op command completed, announced by an interrupt"; the job says "usbd settled".
  Unit3 is the DMAR's INCLUDE_PCI_ALL unit, which covers 00:0d.0 and the AX210 alike (no explicit scope names either:
  a T14 log of main's kernel, scratchpad busywake-r2/metal/t14-head/testcases/kernel.log).
  Reds and what each means:
    "A's compatibility-format message was delivered under IRE" -> exit 1 of issues/qemu-passes-compatibility-format-interrupts.md
       reads red: a kernel-wide isolation defect, filed against every claim, and P1 waits (the owner's Q2).
    "A was not delivered and the unit recorded no reason=0x25" -> blocked without the fault VT-d records: report, do not file as Q2's red.
    a premise red (A not compatibility format, or B not delivered without a fault) -> the instrument failed, nothing is read.
  What A cannot separate: the T14 runs EIME=1 (eime=y in the log above), and VT-d 4.0 section 5.1.4 blocks compatibility format
  where EIME is set OR CFIS is clear. A reads the block under EIME=1 and CFIS=0 together; a reading under CFIS=0 alone needs a
  boot with EIME withheld, which these two do not include.
  By-product, issue's exit 2: a DMA FAULT line in A at all means the unit's fault event (compatibility format, section 5.1.6)
  was delivered with IRE set, since vtd/fault.rs reads the records only in that interrupt's handler.
