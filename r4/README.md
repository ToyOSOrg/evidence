**The T14 ToyOS boot of ada5bf21c, no peer:**
- The `osbench` judge failed on `lan` alone. `osbench_lan` exited 1 after its 60 s discovery:
  `bench=lan refused=no_osbench-peer_answered_on_the_link_within_60s`. The f644cefae hang is gone.
- `crunch` and `persist` ran (toyos/t14-ada5bf21c.txt).

**Table:** compare-t14.md replaces r3's for the T14. It is r3's ToyOS rows plus this boot's `crunch` and
`persist` (toyos/t14-merged.txt), generated by compare.py. The AMD table is r3/compare-amd.md, unchanged.

**Reading the new rows:**
- **crunch:**
  - ToyOS read no temperature, so its phases ran their full length with no stop.
  - Its all-core lines report `mhz` near 3,790. Linux's all-core sha256 and matmul lines report 1,795 and
    1,694 MHz, where Linux ran under intel_pstate powersave at EPP balance_performance.
  - So the all-core ratios (sha256 0.51, matmul 0.39) are largely each system's clock and power policy at
    full load, and ToyOS's held clock is a heat it cannot see.
  - Single-thread: ToyOS is 1.10-1.17x Linux's time for deflate, sha256 and matmul, and 0.68x for parse,
    whose allocations are dlmalloc's against musl's.
- **persist:** not like for like (tests/guest/osbench/src/storage.rs). ToyOS writes the boot USB stick's
  FAT32 log partition through usbd; Linux writes its home on NVMe.
