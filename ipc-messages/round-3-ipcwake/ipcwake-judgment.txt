Judgment:

  4096 and at 65536. h1 does not park between frames.
- Placement split REJECTED: every pair in every boot ran inside llc 0. Base
  ran on cores 0 and 1 (CPUs 1 and 2); h1 on cores 0 and 3 or 3 and 0.
- So by the rule the extra time is in the copies. It scales with bytes:
  +1.9 us per 4096 round trip (two frames) and +35 us per 65536 round trip
  (16 frames, about 2.2 us each).
- The cross-CPU wake count is 0 throughout. It compares a Wake record's CPU
  with the next Pick's, and a Wake is recorded where it is drained, so that
  count measures nothing here and is not used.
