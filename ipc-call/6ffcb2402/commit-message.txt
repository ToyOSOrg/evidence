a call sends a whole message and takes the next one back

SYS_MESSAGE_CALL is SYS_MESSAGE_SEND and then a blocking SYS_MESSAGE_RECV on
the same connection, in one syscall. A refused send answers as a send does
with nothing sent; once the request is queued the receive answers as a
receive does, into the same two windows. A protocol whose peer answers each
request in turn makes the next message its answer. The request carries no
handles: an error could come from either half, so a caller could not know
whether they had moved.

The call no longer tells the scheduler that its caller parks next:
#1111 dropped WakeCause::parks_next (e938fe3e5), and #1129's waker-LLC
affinity carries the waker on every wake instead. What is left is one
syscall entry and exit per round trip.

Consumers: the file server's client (toyos::fs's request and reply) and
osbench's ipc.reqrep rows, whose request's last frame and answer's first
are one call. Connection::call takes the answer straight into the caller's
buffer, as recv_into does. messages_are_whole checks that a call takes the
answer its peer sends, with the answer's handle, split across both windows.
