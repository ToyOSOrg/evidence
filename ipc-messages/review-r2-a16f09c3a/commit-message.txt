review r2: netstack makes both of a link's bells

A NIC driver's hello carried the bell the driver rings, and netstack used
it typed on every receive pass. A handle of another kind in that slot,
even from a plain driver bug, ended the whole stack with a handle fault,
and with it every stream and the healthy second link #1106's failover
keeps. No check settles an event's kind, so netstack cannot refuse it.

The serving side now makes every bell, as blockserve and netstack's own
stream bells already do: Session::open makes both pairs and sends the
driver its waker and its bell with MSG_OPENED (OPENED_HANDLES). The hello
carries only the region, which Region::lent checks, and HELLO_HANDLES
goes. netstack holds only events it made; only the driver trusts received
bells, as diskserver does the block server's. The driver's departure is
still its waker's last handle, so a hang-up keeps its meaning.

Also the review's suggestions: the receive bounds' comment reads, the
pipe-lock record is rewrapped with its page-population sentence on both
copies, a double blank line goes, and response_bytes says its buffer
must hold an error answer too.
