CONTROL, never to land: libc_read_restarts hangs in silence, under a 50 s bound

The guest control for the stalled-program change: the row must name the
program while it stalls and at its guard's expiry, and end without running
`tty_interrupt`. The bound is 50 s so that the control ends inside the
driver's 900 s silence guard on any host: the host scale is at most 8, so
the guard expires by 50 * 8 + 15 = 415 s, and even with the row going on to
its last program the two waits are 830 s.
