What each reading should show (expectations, not results):
- B, kernel log: one `idle: cpuN (processor UID u) waits in C1 or MWAIT 0x31 (exit 253 µs, residency 506 µs), MWAIT 0x60 (exit 1048 µs, residency 2096 µs)` per T14 CPU; on the AMD `port 0x414 (exit 400 µs, residency 800 µs)`. Any `declaration ... refused` line is a finding (CPUID.5 or CStateBaseAddr disagreeing with the tables).
- B, acpiserver: `the processors' idle states: 8 kept, 16 naming no CPU, 0 refused by the kernel; kept: 8 with MWAIT 0x31 253 µs + MWAIT 0x60 1048 µs; ...` on the T14 (the count of CPUs is the MADT's).
- T14 B vs A: cpu.clock max at 4185-4190 MHz where A reads 3789; crunch.single ~1.10x A. sched/time/ipc/tail no worse than A beyond noise. fair/net/procs/mem: if B still reads 3789 there, three or more cores were running at those group starts (the scout read the same with every idle CPU deep), which no idle state lifts.
- T14 M vs B: ipc/tail/time worse (deep exits on every wake, timer lateness up to 1048 µs). If M is no worse, the history and the early wake are untested on metal.
- AMD B vs A: energy group package power, and cpu.clock; no latency regression in sched/time/ipc/tail.
- B testcases: hda_tone green (no underrun with idle CPUs deep); acpi_tables_loaded green, its `[acpi]` line printing the idle census.
